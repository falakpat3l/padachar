# padachar privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

padachar (formerly StrideLocal) is an offline step counter and food log for Android, made by Falak Patel.

## In short

padachar does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything stays on your phone.

## What the app keeps on your phone

- Your steps, distance, calories and walking or running time
- Your food log and any custom food items you add
- The profile details you enter, used only to estimate calories
- Your goals and settings
- If you switch on cycle tracking: the days you log, with flow and symptoms

This data is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## No internet, no tracking

padachar has no internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising ID.

## Permissions and why they are used

- **Physical activity:** to count steps with your phone's step sensor.
- **Run in the background, start after restart and keep awake:** so step counting keeps working through the day.
- **Notifications:** for the optional reminder to move (off by default) and the once-a-day "Daily goal reached" message (can be switched off). padachar only asks for this permission when you switch one of them on in Settings.
- **Health Connect (read steps, distance and step history):** used only when you tap "Import", to bring in your past Google Fit step history. padachar never writes to Health Connect. Imported data stays on your phone.

## Cycle tracking

Cycle tracking is off until you switch it on. Your cycle log is saved on your phone only, like everything else, and is never uploaded or shared. The optional reminder does not show its text on the lock screen. You can delete the whole log in Settings, Cycle tracking.

## Backups

When you make a backup, padachar saves a file to a place you choose. If cycle tracking is on, the backup includes your cycle log unless you switch that off in Settings. That file is yours. Anyone who has it can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

## Deleting your data

In Settings you can delete single days or your oldest days. To delete everything, clear the app's data in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. Backup files you saved are yours to delete. Data inside Health Connect is managed in the Health Connect app.

## Children

padachar is not made for children under 13.
